# Kohuke privacy policy

*Last updated 26 November 2026.*

Kohuke is an iPhone and iPad app for learning Estonian with flashcards. It is
published on the App Store by Avida TECH OÜ, and it has no server of its own:
everything it sends goes to [tallinntastebuds.ee](https://tallinntastebuds.ee),
whose [privacy page](https://tallinntastebuds.ee/privacy) covers what that
site keeps. This page says what the app does.

The data is kept by tallinntastebuds.ee, which Etibar Hasanov runs, and he is
the data controller. For anything about your data, write to
hasanovetibar@gmail.com.

## What stays on your phone

- **The cards, lessons, songs and conversations you have opened**, so they
  can be read without a connection.
- **What you knew and did not know**, while you have no account.
- **Your settings**, such as language. Deleting the app removes all of it.

## What is sent, and when

- **Fetching cards and lessons.** The app asks tallinntastebuds.ee for them,
  with the language you read in. Nothing that says who you are is sent with
  that unless you are signed in.
- **Hearing a word said.** The word or sentence is sent to tallinntastebuds.ee,
  which sends back the sound.
- **Your progress, with an account.** Which cards you knew and which you did
  not is kept under your account, so it follows you to the website and to
  another phone. Starting a deck again deletes what was kept of it.
- **Reporting a card.** The deck, the card and your language are sent, so the
  card can be fixed.
- **Videos.** A lesson with a video plays it from YouTube in its
  privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com), so Google receives your
  phone's internet address when the video plays.

## Accounts

You can use Kohuke without an account. If you make one, it is a username and
a password, or Sign in with Apple or Google, and it is the same account as on
tallinntastebuds.ee. With Apple or Google the site keeps the number Apple or
Google gives for you — never your email address or your name.

**Delete account**, under Me in the app, removes your account and the
progress kept with it, and signs the app out.

## What the app does not do

- No analytics, no advertising and no third-party code in the app.
- No tracking across other apps or websites.
- Nothing is sold or shared for marketing.

## Changes

If what the app sends or keeps changes, this page changes with it, and the
date at the top says when.
