# Kohuke on the App Store: the words of the listing

What goes in the boxes under the version in App Store Connect, kept here so
it is edited in one place and not lost in a browser tab. `docs/RELEASING.md`
says where each box is. The counts are Apple's limits.

Everything below is true of the app as it stands. Two things are left out on
purpose: the hundred and four hundred words the stages open at, because the
website can move them and a listing cannot follow in a day; and the number of
decks, which grows every week.

Apple offers no Azerbaijani listing. It offers Russian, and this text has no
Russian version yet.

## Description (4000)

```
Kohuke is Estonian, one card at a time.

A kohuke is the chocolate-glazed curd bar every shop in Estonia sells: small, sweet and gone in three bites. That is about the size of a sitting here – a few cards on the tram, a lesson over coffee, a song on the way home.

Meanings are in English, Russian and Azerbaijani, and the whole app reads in the one you choose.

WORDS
Decks of Estonian words and phrases, from Tere to what is on the menu. The card shows the Estonian; tap it for what it means, the forms of the word and the word in a sentence. Swipe right for Knew it, left for Show me again. A card you got wrong comes round once more before the run ends, and every card you missed gathers in a deck of its own. Ask for a hint, or undo an answer.

First words and At a restaurant are open from the start. Getting by and Going deeper open as the words you know add up.

HEAR IT
Every word in the app's decks, every form in a lesson and every example sentence can be heard in Estonian.

GRAMMAR
Short lessons on why Estonian words change their endings, with tables of forms and example sentences, and the cases one by one. Many come with a deck to practise them.

SONGS
Estonian songs with their recording and every line translated. Press any word for what it means, its dictionary form and the deck that teaches it.

CONVERSATIONS
A question and the turns that answer it, a sentence at a time, translated, with every word pressable and every sentence spoken.

YOUR OWN DECKS
Signed in, write decks of your own with the words you need.

ONE ACCOUNT, ON THE PHONE AND THE WEB
Kohuke is the flashcards of Tallinn Tastebuds as an app. Sign in with Apple, Google, or a username and password – the same account as tallinntastebuds.ee – and the words you know on one are known on the other. New decks, lessons and songs appear in the app as soon as they are on the website.

Anybody can read the lessons, songs and conversations without an account. Your first five cards are kept on the device; after that an account remembers which ones you knew.

WORKS OFFLINE
Anything you have opened once stays on the device, so a deck can be studied on a plane. Answers given offline are sent when you are back online.

Kohuke is for learning Estonian on your own. No ads and no tracking. Four colour styles. On iPhone and iPad.
```
